\begin{abstract}
We ask whether eight classic daily price and volume signals predict cross-sectional returns on the National Stock Exchange of India after
realistic costs, using a survivorship-free point-in-time panel built from exchange archives, a pre-registered protocol, a trial ledger and a
locked out-of-sample window read once. Development (walk-forward, 2017--2024): long-only top-decile book net information ratio
\R{p1.dev.ir_net} (gross \R{p1.dev.ir_gross}), deflated Sharpe \R{p1.dev.dsr} with \R{honesty.n_all} logged trials.
Holdout (2024-11-01 to 2026-10-30): net IR \R{p1.oos.ir_net}, 95\% bootstrap interval [\R{p1.oos.ir_net.ci95_lo}, \R{p1.oos.ir_net.ci95_hi}];
F\&O long-short net Sharpe \R{p2.oos.sharpe_net}. Every number in this paper is rendered from the run files by \texttt{qr ledger}.
\end{abstract}
