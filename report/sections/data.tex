\section{Data}
Raw files come from the NSE static archives (cash bhavcopy in the legacy and UDiFF formats, delivery files, F\&O bhavcopy, index files),
downloaded at no more than one request per second with an honest User-Agent and an on-disk cache. A permanent security id links symbol changes through
ISIN. NSE's corporate-action export (a manual, cookie-gated download) was not used. Splits and bonuses are instead inferred from the data
(the PRD's fallback, refined): a one-day move above 25\% whose price ratio is within 12\% of a common split, bonus or consolidation ratio is treated as
that action. For stocks without F\&O contracts this is safe, because their price bands (at most 20\%) rule out a genuine move of that size. F\&O
stocks have no band, so the ratio must be confirmed by the implied lot size of a futures contract alive on both days (NSE changes the lot of existing
contracts only for a corporate action) or by open interest; unconfirmed moves are genuine and kept (e.g.\ SBI $+28\%$ on 2017-10-25, Yes Bank $-56\%$ on
2020-03-06), except demerger-like days (open interest up $\ge 1.8\times$ with the lot unchanged), which are nulled. Non-F\&O moves that match no ratio are nulled and logged.
The bhavcopy's own previous-close field turned out not to be split-adjusted, so it could not be used. Dividends are not captured: stock returns are
\emph{price} returns, and the long-only book is therefore compared with the Nifty 500 \emph{price} index. The universe is rebuilt each month from data
dated before the month starts: EQ/BE series, at least 252 sessions of history, at least 55 trades in 63 sessions, close at least Rs 10, median traded value at least
Rs 1 crore, top 750 by value. 
Data-quality gates and the survivorship counts are in the ledger (\texttt{data.*}).
