\section{Robustness}\label{sec:robust}
The cost conclusion does not hinge on one assumption. Table~\ref{tab:sens} re-runs P1 with each cost input moved (same scores, same rules;
these are sensitivities, not new strategies, and are not counted as trials). Removing brokerage entirely or halving the impact coefficient
leaves the net IR negative in both windows; doubling all costs makes it much worse. A 30\% haircut on names that stop trading changes little,
because few held names were delisted.

\paragraph{Leakage checks.} The real-data truncation test rebuilds the whole pipeline from raw files cut at three randomly drawn dates and
requires every price, universe flag, signal and context value dated on or before the cut to be bit-identical to the full build; it passes.
Neither tripwire fired (signal IC above 0.08, development net IR above 2). The first official backtest contained a real bug, a daily rather
than weekly rebalance; it was found from its implausible turnover, fixed with a regression test and re-run, and its two trials remain in the
ledger and in $N_{\mathrm{all}}$ (\texttt{DECISIONS.md} D-033).

\paragraph{Not run.} The PRD's further robustness checks (no sector neutralization, a monthly rebalance, results by calendar year) were not run
before the holdout was read. Running them now would be post-holdout exploration, so they are listed as future work rather than reported.
